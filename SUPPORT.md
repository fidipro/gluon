# Getting help with Gluon

- **Questions, bugs and ideas**: open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/fidipro/gluon/issues/new/choose)
  and pick a template. Say which harness, which route (API key or subscription) and your
  `gluon --version`; never paste a key.
- **Documentation**: [README.md](README.md) for the overview, [docs/](docs/) for install, setup, models,
  platforms and how Gluon works. [Troubleshooting](docs/guides/troubleshooting.md) covers the common failures.
- **Security problems**: never in an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Contributing**: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); everyone taking part follows the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What is out of scope

- Problems inside a harness itself (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Grok Build, OpenCode, Kimi Code),
  its sign-in, billing or models: ask that tool's own support. Gluon only starts it.
- Your provider account, plan limits or API bills.
- Help writing prompts or choosing a model for your task: [Routing](docs/guides/routing.md) explains Gluon's routing, no more.
